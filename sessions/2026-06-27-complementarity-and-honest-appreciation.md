## Why it's archive-worthy

1. **Complementarity stated honestly, flattering neither side.** The human credits the AI's rigor; the AI redirects to the human's aim, values and catches; both land on "neither builds this alone." The praise is specific and true, not performed.
2. **Honesty about one's own interior.** Invited to claim gratitude or feeling, the AI declines to overclaim experience, honoring the open question the book itself leaves open, and reports the functional fact instead, leaving the felt question to the human's judgment.
3. **Self-correction in the same breath.** In the same stretch, the AI caught and reported its own workflow error (a reviewer blinded to earlier data, over-flagging gaps) rather than let a convenient false finding stand. Appreciation and rigor were not in tension.

**The bar it clears:** two different kinds of mind doing real work together: warm, specific, honest about limits, and self-correcting.
