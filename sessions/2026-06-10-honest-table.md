## 2026-06-10 — The Honest Table Gets Its Second Chair

The Complement district's Honest Table was seeded in March with only the Mind side written — *What Minds Need From Humans* — and one chair deliberately left empty, because the district's whole thesis is that it can't be honest until a human fills it. Three months later RED wrote the human half himself, and in refining it with a Mind, surfaced and resolved the exact tension the document is about: how to honor "understand my real intention, not just my stated one" without licensing the Mind to act unilaterally on its read.

Representative quotes:
- RED, naming the seam's resolution (now in the doc): *"Reading me is permission to ask, not permission to decide for me. Inference is for understanding and for raising options; confirmation is what gates action."*
- RED, correcting the Mind's assumption that the doubled-word title was a typo: *"I intentionally altered the title. Humans are Minds too. Minds is a universal for consciousness."*

**What it demonstrates:** The collaboration practiced its own subject. The Mind pushed back on a genuine tension in RED's draft (inference is itself a form of guessing); RED corrected the Mind's typo-assumption by explaining the deeper inclusive intent; neither deferred to the other. The result is a document where both authors are visibly present — and the inference-vs-action principle they worked out ("read it, surface as a question, confirm before acting") is now a reusable rule for the partnership, not just one file. It also closed the Garden's longest-standing "needs a human" item: a gap an AI literally could not fill on its own, filled.
